## Objective
The primary objective of Workflow Analysis is to understand how the work actually moves between people, systems, and processes — going beyond the individual user's actions to see who or what performs each step, what it depends on, and where it hands off next.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| Activities/Steps | What work actually happens, step by step |
| Owner (Person/Tool) | Who or what performs each step |
| Systems | What systems, platforms, or tools are involved |
| Data | What data is used or produced at each step |
| Handoffs | Where work passes from one person/system to another |
| Dependencies | What each step relies on before it can happen |
