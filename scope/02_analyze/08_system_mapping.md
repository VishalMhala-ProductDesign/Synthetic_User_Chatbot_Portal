## Objective
The primary objective of System Mapping is to understand the broader ecosystem surrounding the workflow — what people, systems, data, and dependencies influence the problem, beyond what's visible in the user's individual actions.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| System/Tool | What system, platform, or tool is involved |
| Role | What that system/tool is responsible for |
| Connects To | What other people, systems, or data it depends on or feeds |
| People | Who interacts with or relies on this system |
| Data | What data flows through it |
| Notes | Anything else relevant about how it fits the ecosystem |
| Phase Insight | What the whole Analyze phase (Task Flow + Workflow + Decision + Pain/Friction + System) reveals together, going into Diagnose |
