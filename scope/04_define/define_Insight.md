## Objective
The primary objective of Define Insight is to synthesize what Human–AI Workflow Analysis, AI Capability Analysis, and Agent / AI Skill Analysis reveal together as a single insight - not a summary of each finding, but what the human/AI responsibility split and required capabilities combined mean going into Design.
