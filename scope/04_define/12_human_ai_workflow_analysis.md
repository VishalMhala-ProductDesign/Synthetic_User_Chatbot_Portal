## Objective
The primary objective of Human–AI Workflow Analysis is to define how humans and AI should collaborate — what AI does, what the human does, and how the work hands off between them.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| Step | What happens at this point in the collaborative workflow |
| Performed By (Human/AI) | Who or what carries out this step |
| What Happens | What actually takes place at this step |
| Handoff Notes | How and when the work passes between human and AI |
