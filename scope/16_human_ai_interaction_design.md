## Objective
The primary objective of Human–AI Interaction Design is to design how the human actually interacts with AI at each AI-involved point in the future-state workflow, so the AI's behavior stays understandable and controllable.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| Interaction Point | Where in the workflow the human and AI interact |
| AI Output/Message | What the AI actually communicates to the user |
| User Actions Available | What the user can do in response (e.g. Review, Accept, Reject, Edit) |
| Design Notes | What makes this interaction understandable and controllable |
