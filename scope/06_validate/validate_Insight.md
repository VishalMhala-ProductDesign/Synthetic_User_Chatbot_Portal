
## Validate Insight Input
| Validate Insight | Input to Validate Insight |
|--|--|
| Validate Insight | Output of Validation & Usability Analysis and Outcome / KPI Analysis |

## Objective
The primary objective of Validate Insight is to synthesize what Validation & Usability Analysis and Outcome / KPI Analysis reveal together as a single insight - not a summary of each finding, but what the usability findings and KPI results combined mean going into Product Design Insight.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| What Validation & Usability Analysis Reveals | The core usability insight |
| What Outcome / KPI Analysis Reveals | The core KPI insight |
| Pattern | The theme that repeats across both |
| Phase Insight | The single synthesized insight the whole Validate phase reveals, going into Product Design Insight |
| Working Design Hypothesis | A preliminary, testable design idea this phase's insight points toward - provisional, to be refined or discarded as later phases add evidence |

## Output
**What Validation & Usability Analysis reveals:** AI identifing the core usability insight.

**What Outcome / KPI Analysis reveals:** AI identifing the core KPI insight.

Pattern across both - the evidence repeatedly focuses on:
- AI identifing pattern theme

**Validate — Phase Insight:**

> AI identifing the single synthesized insight the Validation & Usability Analysis and Outcome / KPI Analysis evidence reveals together as a whole - not a restatement of any one row, but what the usability findings and KPI results combined mean for Product Design Insight.

**Working Design Hypothesis:** AI identifing a preliminary, testable design idea this phase's insight points toward - explicitly provisional, to be refined or discarded as later phases add evidence. Not a final Design Implication (that's Product Design Insight's job at the end of the chain) - a running hypothesis to carry forward.
