## Objective
The primary objective of Future-State Workflow is to redesign the original workflow using the agreed AI skills and human–AI responsibilities, showing what the improved, end-to-end workflow should look like.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| Step | What happens at this point in the redesigned workflow |
| Performed By (Human/AI) | Who or what carries out this step in the future state |
| What Happens (Future State) | What actually takes place at this step after the redesign |
| What Changed vs Current | How this step differs from how it works today |
