## Objective
The primary objective of Design Insight is to synthesize what Future-State Workflow, Human–AI Interaction Design, and Trust & Control Analysis reveal together as a single insight - not a summary of each finding, but what the redesigned experience and its trust/control mechanisms combined mean going into Validate.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| What Future-State Workflow Reveals | The core redesigned-workflow insight |
| What Human–AI Interaction Design Reveals | The core interaction-model insight |
| What Trust & Control Analysis Reveals | The core trust/control insight |
| Pattern | The theme that repeats across all three |
| Phase Insight | The single synthesized insight the whole Design phase reveals, going into Validate |
| Working Design Hypothesis | A preliminary, testable design idea this phase's insight points toward - provisional, to be refined or discarded as later phases add evidence |
