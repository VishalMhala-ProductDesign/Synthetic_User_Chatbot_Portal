
## Diagnose Insight Input
| Diagnose Insight | Input to Diagnose Insight |
|--|--|
| Diagnose Insight | Output of Root Cause Analysis, Opportunity Analysis, and AI Opportunity Analysis |

## Objective
The primary objective of Diagnose Insight is to synthesize what Root Cause Analysis, Opportunity Analysis, and AI Opportunity Analysis reveal together as a single insight - not a summary of each finding, but what the root cause and AI fit combined mean going into Define.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| What Root Cause Analysis Reveals | The core root-cause insight |
| What Opportunity Analysis Reveals | The core opportunity insight |
| What AI Opportunity Analysis Reveals | The core AI-fit insight |
| Pattern | The theme that repeats across all three |
| Phase Insight | The single synthesized insight the whole Diagnose phase reveals, going into Define |
| Working Design Hypothesis | A preliminary, testable design idea this phase's insight points toward - provisional, to be refined or discarded as later phases add evidence |
