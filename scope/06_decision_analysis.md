## Objective
The primary objective of Decision Analysis is to understand where, why, and how a decision is made — identifying the decision maker, the inputs and criteria they weigh, the options they consider, and the outcome they choose.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| Decision Point | Where a choice or judgment has to be made |
| Decision Maker | Who or what makes the decision |
| Inputs | What information feeds the decision |
| Criteria | What standard or reasoning the decision is based on |
| Options Considered | What alternatives were available |
| Outcome/Choice | What was actually decided |
| Why | The reasoning behind the choice |
