## Objective
The primary objective of Product Design Insight is to interpret what the Outcome / KPI results actually reveal about the product design — not to simply repeat the KPI, but to translate each outcome into a design insight, surface the pattern across all KPIs, and convert that pattern into concrete design implications.

## Helps to Identify
| Element | What you learn |
|--|--|
| KPI | Which KPI this insight is derived from |
| What the Outcome Tells Us | What the KPI result reveals about user or system behavior |
| Product Design Insight | What that outcome means for the product design |
| Design Implication (per KPI) | A concrete, actionable design change that KPI's insight points to |
| Evidence | Which qualities the KPI rows collectively show the product must balance |
| Pattern | The cross-KPI pattern once every KPI is viewed together |
| Design Implication (overall) | A concrete, actionable design change the overall pattern points to |
